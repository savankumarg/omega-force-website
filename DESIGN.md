## Brand & Style

The visual narrative balances high-end mastercraft professionalism with the dependable reality of urgent, high-stakes home services. Moving away from generic contractor tropes or tech-heavy SaaS styling, the aesthetic treats residential appliance infrastructure with architectural seriousness and tailored precision.

The tone is authoritative, composed, and swift. Visual weight is concentrated in deep structural anchors, crisp technical dividers, and measured bursts of decisive action color. It communicates quiet confidence to homeowners who demand licensed, meticulous, and accountable care for premium household machines.

## Colors

The palette establishes an architectural hierarchy rooted in deep structural navy, balanced by pristine light environments:

- **Primary Canvas & Foundation (`#142533`):** The grounding deep navy used for authoritative headings, solid structural headers, dark trust strips, high-contrast text, and architectural borders.
- **Secondary Environment (`#087FC3`):** Precision technical blue reserved for brand environments, selected interactive states, navigation underlines, subtle focus rings, and verified indicators.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#E31837`):** High-urgency signal red reserved strictly for immediate call-to-actions, emergency booking indicators, direct dispatch status, and critical alerts. Never diluted across decorative backdrops.
- **Neutral Core (`#60717D`):** Balanced industrial slate for metadata, secondary descriptions, operational hours, and muted structural guidelines.
- **Surface System:** Crisp pure white (`#FFFFFF`) for elevated cards, modal panels, and floating booking consoles, resting on a warm off-white canvas (`#FAFBFC`). A delicate soft blue wash (`#EAF5FB`) anchors trust sections, diagnostic containers, and warrantee callouts.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy juxtaposes the direct structural momentum of Chivo against the refined geometric balance of Manrope:

- **Display & Headline Levels (Chivo):** Engineered for confidence and authoritative trade presence. Chivo carries a confident, industrial cut that establishes immediate competence. Tighter tracking on larger scales keeps titles grounded and punchy, preventing sprawling whitespace in tight editorial columns.
- **Body & Label Levels (Manrope):** Chosen for supreme screen legibility and calculated geometry. It balances technical precision with warm clarity, ensuring diagnostic steps, pricing estimates, and customer service reviews remain effortlessly scannable across desktop displays and field mobile devices.

## Layout & Spacing

A 12-column layout grid structures all standard desktop views, shifting to a 6-column grid for tablets, and a 4-column layout on mobile.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** Max-width containment of `1280px` centered with `margin: 3rem`. Gutter width remains anchored at `1.5rem` to enforce tight, technical proximity between diagnostic cards, booking modules, and proof points.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** Fluid margins at `2rem`, scaling columns down to 6 while preserving gutters at `1.5rem`.
- **Mobile (<768px):** Tight outer boundary padding at `1.25rem` (`margin-mobile`), collapsing components to full-width linear stacks with `space-md` (`1rem`) vertical flow spacing.
- **Rhythm Principle:** Internal cards maintain `space-lg` padding to avoid cramped content, while paired micro-metadata (icons + labels, technician ratings + counts) rely strictly on `space-xs` and `space-sm` for immediate spatial association.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects exaggerated drop shadows in favor of a crisp architectural approach combining subtle tinted shadows with structural borders:

- **Surface Base:** Flat `#FAFBFC` canvas with zero baseline elevation.
- **Structural Elevation (Cards, Panels):** Surfaces use pure `#FFFFFF` bounded by a razor-thin 1px border in `#E6EDF2`. Ambient depth is achieved with a single diffused navy shadow: `0 8px 30px rgba(20, 37, 51, 0.06)`.
- **Raised Interactive Layer (Drawers, Sticky Booking Strips):** Elevated elements introduce a firmer secondary shadow footprint: `0 12px 40px rgba(20, 37, 51, 0.10)` alongside a 1px `#142533` tinted edge at 8% opacity.
- **Overlay Layer (Emergency Modals, Dispatch Status Overlays):** Deep navy scrim (`rgba(20, 37, 51, 0.65)`) paired with high-elevation surface containment: `0 24px 60px rgba(20, 37, 51, 0.18)`.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a structured, purposeful profile. Radii are intentionally kept controlled and architectural:

- **Base Radii (`roundedness: 1`):** Base components such as input fields, inline select boxes, and badges utilize `0.25rem` (`4px`) to preserve structural stability.
- **Action Radii:** Interactive controls and trigger buttons scale to `0.5rem` (`8px`) for immediate affordance and ergonomic finger targets.
- **Container Radii:** Standard presentation cards and diagnostic containers sit at `0.75rem` (`12px`).
- **Feature Radii:** Hero imagery, interactive diagnostic visualizers, and brand showcases utilize a maximum corner envelope of `1rem` (`16px`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Emergency / Primary Action:** Solid `#E31837` fill, white `#FFFFFF` text, `0.5rem` border radius, `font-weight: 700`. Subtle hover state shifts to `#C5112D` without visual jump. Internal padding is strictly `0.875rem 1.75rem`.
- **Secondary Trade Action:** Deep navy `#142533` fill, white `#FFFFFF` text. Focus state provides an outward `2px` ring in `#087FC3`.
- **Ghost / Outlined:** Transparent fill, `1.5px` border in `#142533` or `#087FC3`, text matched to border color. Background tints to `#EAF5FB` at 50% opacity on active hover.

### Cards & Trust Panels
- **Standard Card:** `#FFFFFF` background, `0.75rem` corner radius, `1px solid #E6EDF2`, elevated with `0 8px 30px rgba(20, 37, 51, 0.06)`. Internal padding scales to `1.5rem`.
- **Trust Strip Card:** Tinted `#EAF5FB` container with no shadow, bounded by a 1px `#087FC3` line at 20% opacity. Features bold statistics in `#142533` paired with muted captions in `#60717D`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- **Text Inputs:** Height of `48px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E6EDF2`, radius `0.25rem`. Text input color is `#142533` with placeholder set to `#60717D`.
- **Active / Focused Input:** Border shifts instantly to `1.5px solid #087FC3` accompanied by a `0 0 0 3px rgba(8, 127, 195, 0.12)` halo.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Selection Units:** `20px` square (or circle), `1.5px solid #60717D` on neutral canvas.
- **Checked State:** Fill `#087FC3` with crisp white `#FFFFFF` checkmark or radio dot. Focus produces a `2px` offset ring.

### Chips & Verification Badges
- **Status Chips:** Height `26px`, radius `0.25rem`, padding `0 0.625rem`. 
- **Licensed / Certified Badge:** `#EAF5FB` background, `#087FC3` text, `label-md` uppercase styling, accompanied by a micro `#087FC3` shield or check icon.
- **Urgent / Same-Day Badge:** Soft red wash (`#FDF0F2`), `#E31837` bold text, displaying immediate dispatch status.
